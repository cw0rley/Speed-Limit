Speed Limit - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Overview

Speed Limit ("the App") shows your current driving speed and the posted speed limit for the road you are on, on your iPhone or Android phone and, on iPhone, on your Lock Screen and the Apple CarPlay Dashboard. The App is published by Patrick Cunningham ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the App uses, where it goes, and how long it is kept.

In short: we don't sell your data, we don't show ads, we don't use analytics or tracking, and the App has no user accounts. Your location is used only to show your speed and the speed limit, and we don't keep a record of where you have been.

Location

The App uses your device's GPS to calculate your speed and to find the road you are on. Your speed is calculated on your device and is never sent anywhere.

To look up speed limits, the App sends location information to the services below:

  • OpenStreetMap map data (Overpass API). The App requests the roads in an area of roughly one mile around you, by sending the corners of that area. The roads are then matched to your position on your device. These requests go directly from your device to public Overpass servers (currently overpass-api.de and overpass.kumi.systems), which are operated by third parties.
  • TomTom, through our server. When OpenStreetMap has no speed limit for your road, the App may send your approximate position (rounded to about 10 meters) and direction of travel to our server, which asks TomTom for the speed limit and returns the answer to your device. TomTom receives the position and direction but not your IP address or any identifier. Our server does not store your position.

The App does not store a location history on your device or anywhere else.

Background location and CarPlay

On iPhone, when you turn on Show on CarPlay, the App keeps using your location while it is in the background so it can keep your speed and the speed limit up to date on the Lock Screen, in the Dynamic Island, and on the CarPlay Dashboard. iOS shows a blue location indicator while this is on. It stops when you turn it off, and the App turns it off automatically after about 10 minutes without movement. Location is used in the background only for the purposes described in this policy.

The App displays information on CarPlay but does not read any data from your vehicle.

Our server

Speed-limit requests to TomTom go through a small server we run on Cloudflare Workers. To keep usage within limits and prevent abuse, the server records:

  • your IP address with a count of requests for the current hour, deleted within about 48 hours;
  • a total count of requests per day, with no link to any device or person.

When subscriptions are required, the App may also send an anonymous subscription identifier (see below) so the server can confirm you have an active subscription. The server does not store your location or the speed limits it returns. As our hosting provider, Cloudflare processes these requests on our behalf.

Subscriptions and purchases

Subscriptions are sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles your payment. We never see or receive your name, email address, or payment details.

We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous identifier created by the App, along with your purchase and subscription status from the store, your device platform and app version, and your IP address. We use this information only to unlock subscription features, restore purchases, and handle billing questions.

Data stored on your device

  • Your settings (unit and speed-alert thresholds).
  • Lookup statistics shown in Settings: how long speed limits were available while driving, and how many map and speed-limit requests were made. These are totals only, with no locations, and they are not sent anywhere. You can reset them in Settings.
  • Whether you have dismissed the subscription announcement.

Deleting the App removes this data.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, analytics, or tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data for marketing.
  • No user accounts, and no collection of your name, email address, or contacts.

Your choices

  • You can turn location access off, or change it, in your device's Settings. The App cannot show your speed or speed limits without it.
  • You can turn off Show on CarPlay at any time to stop background location.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store or Google Play account settings.
  • To ask about, or request deletion of, data related to you, contact us at the address below. Because we don't keep location history or accounts, there is usually very little to delete. RevenueCat's subscription records can be deleted on request.

Children's privacy

The App is intended for drivers and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

All requests the App makes are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The TomTom API key is kept on our server and is never stored in the App.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. If a change is significant, we will also mention it in the App's release notes.
